ACME CORP INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT (REVISED 2026)

HEALTH AND BENEFITS POLICY:
All full-time employees at ACME Corp are entitled to up to $1,500 per calendar year in dental reimbursements. Requests must be submitted via the HR portal by December 15th of each year.

OFFICE STIPEND POLICY:
Remote engineers receive a one-time workspace setup stipend of $500. This stipend covers hardware additions such as monitors, ergonomic chairs, and keyboard peripherals. Receipts must be uploaded to the ExpenseApp within 30 days of purchase.

CODE REVIEWS AND SECURITY:
All production code updates must be peer-reviewed by at least two senior engineers before branch merging. Production keys must never be committed to git.
